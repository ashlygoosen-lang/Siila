About the Critical Skills List

The list is published by the Minister of Home Affairs under section 19(4) of the Immigration Act 13 of 2002. The current list is Government Notice R.3934 in Government Gazette 49402 of 3 October 2023. It has 142 occupations, each with an OFO code, a minimum qualification (NQF level) and, usually, registration with a professional body recognised by SAQA.

For jobs not on the list, employers can sponsor a General Work Visa, which needs 100 points under the points-based system or a recommendation from the Department of Employment and Labour. On TalentBridge, a long-term job is open only to South African citizens and permanent residents for the first 90 days. How international hiring works.

This page is a guide, not legal advice. The Department of Home Affairs decides every visa application, and the list can change.
