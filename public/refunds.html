The short version

  • Money paid into Escrow stays there until you approve the work, so you are protected before anything is paid out.
  • If the Professional cancels, or you both agree to cancel, you get a full refund.
  • If the work is not as agreed, open a dispute before approving it. Funds stay in Escrow while we decide.
  • You may cancel within 7 days of the Offer being accepted for a full refund, as long as the work has not started with your consent.
  • Refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within 5 business days of the decision, plus bank processing time.
  • Once you approve the work and the money is released, it can no longer be refunded through TalentBridge.

This summary helps you find your way around. The full text below is what applies.

1. Scope

This Refund Policy explains when and how money paid into Escrow on TalentBridge is refunded to Clients. It forms part of our Terms of Use, and words defined there have the same meaning here.

TalentBridge holds the Contract price in Escrow between the Client and the Professional. Because the Professional is only paid once the Client approves the work, most problems can be solved before any money leaves Escrow.

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 or the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002.

3. Cooling-off right

Under section 44 of ECTA, a consumer may cancel an agreement for services concluded electronically, without reason and without penalty, within 7 days after the agreement was concluded.

This right does not apply to services that began with the consumer’s consent before the end of the 7-day period (ECTA section 42(2)). If you want the Professional to start immediately, you are consenting to that, and the cooling-off right ends once the work starts. If you cancel during the cooling-off period, you will receive a full refund of the amount paid into Escrow, within 30 days as ECTA requires and usually much sooner.

To cancel, use the inbox to tell the Professional and email [support email] with the Contract number.

4. How to request a refund

  1. Talk to the Professional. Many issues are solved in the inbox, and the Professional may agree to cancel.
  2. Open a dispute from the Contract page if you cannot agree. Explain what went wrong and attach evidence such as photos, documents or messages. Funds stay in Escrow.
  3. We review the Contract, messages and evidence from both sides, and may ask for more information. Please respond within 5 business days.
  4. We decide, usually within 10 business days of receiving the evidence, and tell both parties by email and on the Platform.

Disputes should be opened before the Client approves the work. Once Escrow is released, the funds have been paid to the Professional.

5. How and when refunds are paid

  • Refunds go back to the original payment method wherever possible.
  • Card and instant EFT: we start the refund within 5 business days of the decision. Your bank may take a further 5 to 10 business days to show it.
  • Manual EFT: we pay into a South African bank account in the name of the Client who paid, within 5 business days after receiving confirmation of the account. We may ask for proof of the account to prevent fraud.
  • We refund the full amount paid into Escrow. No Service Fee is charged to the Client, so none is deducted, and we do not deduct payment processing costs from a refund.
  • All refunds are paid in South African rand (ZAR). Any currency conversion or foreign card charges applied by your bank are outside our control.

6. Partial refunds and settlements

If the work is partly done, the Client and the Professional may agree to split the Escrow, for example where some work was delivered and accepted. Tell us the agreed split in the dispute, and both parties must confirm it in writing on the Platform. We will then refund the Client’s share and release the Professional’s share, less the Service Fee on the amount released.

7. For Professionals

  • If a Contract is refunded in full, no Service Fee is charged to you.
  • If Escrow is refunded because of a dispute decision against you, it may affect your standing on the Platform, but we will not take money from you beyond the Escrow except to recover amounts paid to you in error or through fraud.
  • If a payout was made in error, you must repay it, and we may deduct it from future payouts.

8. What is not refundable through TalentBridge

  • Escrow funds that have already been released after the Client approved the work.
  • Payments made outside the Platform, which are not protected by Escrow.
  • Losses beyond the amount paid into Escrow, such as materials bought separately or loss of income. These may be claimed from the other party under the law.

9. Chargebacks

Please contact us or open a dispute before asking your bank to reverse a card payment. A chargeback freezes the related funds while the bank investigates and can delay a fair outcome for both parties. If a chargeback is made in bad faith, for example after approving the work, we may suspend the account and recover the amount.

10. Fraud and abuse

We may refuse a refund, or reverse one, where we reasonably believe it was obtained by fraud, misrepresentation or abuse of this policy. Repeated or unfounded refund requests may lead to account restrictions under our Terms.

11. Contact and escalation

For refund questions, email [support email] or call [support phone], quoting your Contract number. We acknowledge requests within 2 business days.

If you are a consumer and are not satisfied with how we handled your refund, you may contact the Consumer Goods and Services Ombud (cgso.org.za, 0860 000 272, info@cgso.org.za) or the National Consumer Commission.
