7. International hiring and work-visa sponsorship

TalentBridge puts South African citizens and permanent residents first. The rules below let Clients hire foreign nationals for long-term work only when no suitable local person is available.

Who may work on the Platform

  • South African citizens and permanent residents may apply for any Job. Foreign nationals who already hold a South African visa or permit that allows the work may also apply for any Job, once we have checked that document.
  • Foreign nationals who need a work visa may apply only for remote Jobs (work done from outside South Africa) and for Jobs we have opened to international applicants. They must not work in South Africa until a valid visa has been issued.

Opening a Job to international applicants

  • Only full-time Jobs that are permanent or have a fixed term of 12 months or more qualify. The Job must have been advertised on the Platform to South African citizens and permanent residents for at least 90 days, the Client must be verified, and nobody may have been hired for it.
  • The Client must tell us truthfully how many South African applicants they considered and why none was suitable, and the salary offered. We review every request and may decline it, ask for more information, or close an approved Job if we believe local applicants were not fairly considered.
  • Where the occupation is on the Critical Skills List published by the Minister of Home Affairs, the Critical Skills Work Visa usually applies. Otherwise the General Work Visa applies, which needs enough points under the points-based system or a recommendation from the Department of Employment and Labour.

The Client is the employer and sponsor

  • A sponsored job offer creates an employment relationship between the Client and the professional, not a Contract paid through Escrow. TalentBridge is not the employer, sponsor or immigration practitioner, and is not a party to the employment contract.
  • The Client must offer terms at least equal to South African labour law, must not employ the person before a valid visa is issued (section 38 of the Immigration Act 13 of 2002), and must meet the employer’s undertakings Home Affairs requires, including keeping the employee’s passport valid and telling Home Affairs if the employment ends.
  • The visa application can be handled by GlobalPath Immigration, a division of VINK Group (Pty) Ltd like TalentBridge, for its own fee, or by any registered immigration practitioner the Client chooses. Using GlobalPath is optional.
  • We do not guarantee that a visa will be issued, or how long it will take. The Department of Home Affairs decides every application, and requirements can change.

Bids

Offers and acceptance

A Client may send an Offer, either by awarding a Bid or directly to a Professional. The Offer states the price, the scope and any Milestones. The Contract page records the price, the Service Fee and VAT on that fee, and the amount the Professional will receive, before any payment is made.

A Client may withdraw an Offer before it is accepted. A Professional may decline an Offer.

Performing the Contract

  • The Professional must perform the work with reasonable care and skill, in the agreed time, and in line with the scope, the law and any applicable industry standards.
  • The Client must provide the information, access and co-operation reasonably needed, and must review delivered work promptly.

Milestones

Milestones record progress. The Professional marks each Milestone complete and the Client approves it. Unless the Contract says otherwise, the Escrow is released once, when the Client approves the whole Contract.
