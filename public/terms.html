Posting Jobs

Bids

A Bid is an invitation to deal, not a binding offer. Professionals must bid only on work they are qualified and available to do, and may edit or withdraw a Bid until they are hired. Clients may shortlist, decline or chat with bidders.

Offers and acceptance

A Client may send an Offer, either by awarding a Bid or directly to a Professional. The Offer states the price, the scope and any Milestones. A Contract is formed when the Professional accepts the Offer on the Platform. The Contract page records the price, the Service Fee and VAT on that fee, and the amount the Professional will receive, before any payment is made.

A Client may withdraw an Offer before it is accepted. A Professional may decline an Offer.

Performing the Contract

  • The Professional must perform the work with reasonable care and skill, in the agreed time, and in line with the scope, the law and any applicable industry standards.
  • The Client must provide the information, access and co-operation reasonably needed, and must review delivered work promptly.
  • Changes to scope, price or deadlines should be agreed in writing in the Platform inbox. Where the price changes, the parties should complete the current Contract or cancel it and agree a new one.

Milestones

Milestones record progress. The Professional marks each Milestone complete and the Client approves it. Unless the Contract says otherwise, the Escrow is released once, when the Client approves the whole Contract.

9. Disputes between Clients and Professionals

  1. Talk first. Use the Platform inbox to raise the problem and try to agree a solution. Keep all communication on the Platform.
  2. Open a dispute. While Escrow is funded or work has been delivered, either party may open a dispute from the Contract page and explain what went wrong. Funds stay in Escrow while the dispute is open.
  3. Evidence. We may ask both parties for evidence, such as photos, files, the scope and messages. Please respond within 5 business days.
  4. Decision. We review the Contract, the messages and the evidence, and decide in good faith whether to release the Escrow to the Professional or refund the Client. We aim to decide within 10 business days of receiving the evidence. If both parties agree a partial settlement, we will give effect to it.

Our decision determines what happens to the Escrow funds only. It is not a court judgment and does not stop either party from pursuing their legal rights against the other. TalentBridge is not responsible for any amount awarded by a court or arbitrator against a user.
