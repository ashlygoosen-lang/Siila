The short version

  • We collect what we need to run the marketplace: your account, profile, jobs, bids, contracts, messages and payment references.

4. Who we share it with

  • Other users. Your public profile (name, headline, skills, rate, location, ratings, reviews, portfolio) is visible to visitors. When you bid, message or contract with someone, they see the information needed to work with you, such as your Bid, messages, files and the Contract. Your email address and phone number are not shown publicly.
  • Service providers (operators). Hosting and infrastructure, email delivery, payment processing and backups. They may process personal information only on our instructions and must keep it secure, under written agreements as POPIA section 21 requires.
  • Professional advisers such as auditors, accountants and lawyers, under duties of confidentiality.
  • Authorities. Law-enforcement, regulators (including the South African Revenue Service and the Financial Intelligence Centre) or courts, when the law requires it or to protect rights, property or safety.
  • Business transfers. A buyer or successor if the TalentBridge business is sold or restructured, subject to this policy.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

5. International transfers

Our hosting and some service providers may store or process information on servers outside South Africa, including in the United States and the European Union. When we transfer personal information outside South Africa, we do so only as POPIA section 72 allows, for example where the recipient is subject to laws, binding corporate rules or a contract providing protection substantially similar to POPIA, or where the transfer is necessary to perform our contract with you.

For users in the EU or UK, transfers rely on adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses where required.

7. Your rights

Under POPIA (and the GDPR, where it applies) you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you. Use Download my data on your Account page for an instant copy;
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information, most of which you can edit yourself;
  • Delete your information, using Delete account on the Account page, subject to records we must keep by law;
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time;
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on consent;
  • Restrict processing and data portability (GDPR);
  • Not be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing that significantly affect you;
  • Complain to a supervisory authority.

To make a request, email [support email]. We may need to verify your identity. We respond within 30 days and do not charge for reasonable requests. Requests for records may also be made under our Promotion of Access to Information Act (PAIA) manual, available on request.

Information Regulator (South Africa). You may lodge a complaint through the Regulator’s eServices portal or contact it at inforegulator.org.za, enquiries@inforegulator.org.za, toll-free 0800 017 160. EU and UK users may contact their local data protection authority.

California and other US state residents. You have the right to know, delete and correct personal information and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

8. How we protect it

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all traffic.
  • Passwords stored only as salted one-way hashes (bcrypt). We never see your password.
  • Signed, expiring login tokens. Verification and password-reset links expire.
  • Rate limiting and validation of all input to resist abuse.
  • Uploaded files checked for their real file type, and served only to authorised users.
  • Role-based access to administrative tools, with an audit log of sensitive actions.
  • Regular backups and controlled access to servers.

No system is completely secure. If a security compromise affects your personal information, we will notify the Information Regulator and you as soon as reasonably possible, as POPIA section 22 requires, and tell you what you can do to protect yourself.

9. Cookies and similar technology

We keep tracking to a minimum:

  • Essential storage. When you log in, a login token is saved in your browser’s local storage so you stay signed in. Logging out removes it.
  • No advertising or cross-site tracking. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or third-party analytics that follow you across websites.
  • Fonts and images are served from our own servers.

If we introduce optional analytics or other non-essential cookies in future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

10. Direct marketing

We send service messages that are necessary to run your account. We send marketing only if you have agreed, or about similar TalentBridge services if you are an existing customer, in line with POPIA section 69. Every marketing message includes a free way to opt out, and you can also opt out by emailing [support email].

11. Children

The Platform is only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Information you receive from other users

If you receive another user’s personal information through the Platform (for example an address for on-site work), you are responsible for using it only for that Job and for protecting it under POPIA. Do not copy it into other systems or use it for marketing.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will tell you about material changes by email or on the Platform before they take effect. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
