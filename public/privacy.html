(“SIIRA”) is the responsible party under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). This policy explains what we collect and why. Information Officer: , .

What we collect

Account details (name, email, mobile number, province); profile content; jobs, applications, contracts and messages; payment references; the last 4 characters of an ID or certificate number and, temporarily, the document you upload for verification; basic technical logs for security.

Why we use it

To run your account and the marketplace, connect clients and professionals, verify identity, process and protect payments, resolve disputes, send service emails, prevent fraud, meet tax and legal duties, and improve the service. We rely on your consent, on our contract with you, on legal obligations and on our legitimate interest in keeping the Platform safe.

Who sees it

Other users see the profile details you choose to publish and the information needed to work together on a contract. We share data with service providers who help us run SIIRA, such as payment providers, email delivery and hosting, and with authorities where the law requires. We do not sell personal information.

Where it is stored

Our hosting provider may store data on servers outside South Africa. We take reasonable steps to make sure the recipient protects it to a standard similar to POPIA.

How long we keep it

Verification documents are deleted once reviewed. Financial and contract records are kept for at least five years as the law requires. Other data is kept while your account is active and then deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

You can access your information (download it from your dashboard), correct it, ask us to delete it, object to processing, and withdraw consent. To use a right, use your dashboard or email us. You may complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

Security

Passwords are stored hashed, traffic uses HTTPS, access to admin tools is restricted and logged, and we keep backups. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify you and the Regulator if a breach affects you, as the law requires.

Cookies and local storage

SIIRA stores a login token in your browser so you stay signed in. We do not use advertising trackers.

Children

SIIRA is for people 18 and older.
