Questions, answered

Is it free to join?

Yes. Creating a profile, posting jobs, bidding and messaging are free. The only charge is a service fee taken from the professional’s payout when a contract is paid.

How do bids work?

Professionals send a price, how many days they need and a short proposal. Clients can sort bids, shortlist, chat with bidders and award the job to the best fit.

How does escrow work?

When an offer is accepted, the client pays the full price into escrow. TalentBridge holds it while the work is done and releases it to the professional when the client approves.

How do I get a verified badge?

Upload a South African ID, passport, trade certificate or CIPC document from your dashboard. Our team checks it, keeps only the last four characters of the number and deletes the file after review.

Can I split a project into milestones?

Yes. When you send an offer you can add up to ten milestones. The professional marks each one complete and you approve it, so progress is clear on both sides.

What if the work isn’t right?

Funds stay in escrow while a dispute is open. Our team reviews the conversation and contract, then releases the payment or refunds the client.

Can I message a professional before hiring?

Yes. Clients can message any professional from their profile or chat with anyone who bid on their job, and share photos and documents in the chat.

Do I get an invoice?

Every completed contract comes with an invoice for the service fee and a payment statement, which you can download from your dashboard.

Which areas do you cover?

All nine provinces. Filter jobs and professionals by province, or search by skill.

How is my information protected?

TalentBridge is built around POPIA. You can download all your data or delete your account at any time from your dashboard.
