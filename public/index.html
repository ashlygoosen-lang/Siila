Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before you get started.

How do I get a verified badge?

Upload a South African ID and any trade certificates. Our team checks them, and approved profiles show the verified badge on every proposal and search result.

Is it free to create a profile?

Yes. Creating a profile and browsing opportunities is free, so you can see how SIIRA works before you commit to anything.

How are payments protected?

For projects, clients fund each milestone up front and the money is released when the work is approved, so both sides are covered.

Can I hire for a single day?

Yes. Post a short-term job, set a day rate and local professionals can accept it, often the same day.

Which areas does SIIRA cover?

Opportunities are listed across all nine provinces, and you can filter by province or search by skill.
